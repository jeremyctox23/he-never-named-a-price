What he never did

  • Never named his price.He named his outcome. The retainer and the percentage were never spoken.
  • Never demoed anything.His product got about a minute, described as a scope, a price and a milestone.
  • Never promised a result.Best effort, and a timeline he made longer than they expected.
  • Never asked for the yes.He asked for a list and set a week.

Source: the recording of one 60-minute call, used with Lior Weinstein's permission. The client is not named or shown, and their replies are cut: the picture holds on Lior. Quotes are his words with filler and false starts removed. Minute marks are approximate.

Build your own deal

For anyone who brings a skill into a company and makes it grow. Qualify the deal, set the numbers, and leave with a one-page outline and a call plan in your own words.

Step 1 · Is this a chief deal or a vendor job?

Step 2 · Your numbers

A retainer, plus a share of what the company earns above the growth line it sets for itself, with nothing paid during the cliff. Move the sliders to your deal.

  • On their own, at the baseline they set
  • With you
  • Above the baseline. The only money your share comes out of.
  • The cliff. No share is paid here.
